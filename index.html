Chapter 1
The Stripped Uniform

The morning of Garter Day at Ashford Castle began in a flurry of damp wind and iron-gray skies. In Britain, Garter Day is one of the most ancient ceremonies in the realm, where knights and royals gather in dark velvet mantles and hats tipped with white ostrich plumes to parade down the castle hill to the chapel. For the adults, it was a day of solemn vows. For eight-year-old Prince Albie, it was something far more important: it was the first time he had ever been allowed to wear the miniature uniform of the Castle Guard.

The uniform was a work of patient art. Cut from scarlet wool with gold braid along the collar, it had been fitted by the castle tailor over three careful afternoons. On the left breast was pinned the tiny silver badge of the Order—an honor traditionally given to young royal pages on ceremonial days. Albie stood on a wooden footstool in the Great Vestry, his shiny black shoes catching the lamplight, puffing his small chest out until he looked like a miniature guardsman.

His mother, Princess Clara, knelt in front of him, smoothing the stiff wool collar. She wore an elegant gown of pale moss green, her dark hair pinned up beneath a wide-brimmed hat. Though she had only returned to full public duties after her long treatment, her face held a soft, untroubled glow that had become beloved across the country.

"Stand still for just two seconds, darling," Clara whispered, smiling as she straightened the gold buttons. "Or you will march like a penguin."
